Tutoring Services Agreement

Student & Parent Enrollment Agreement

1. Session Scheduling & Attendance

Tutoring sessions will occur at the agreed scheduled time. Students are expected to arrive on time with all necessary materials, assignments, and study tools.

2. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled session. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice or missed sessions ("no-shows") will be billed at the full session rate.

3. Academic Integrity

Tutoring is designed to support learning, build critical skills, and enhance comprehension. The tutor will guide the student through course concepts, but will not complete homework assignments, tests, or graded projects on behalf of the student.

4. Payment Terms

Invoices are issued weekly or monthly according to your selected plan. Payment must be received prior to or on the day of the first session of each billing period.

5. Electronic Signature Consent

By signing below, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accept all terms set forth in this agreement. This electronic signature carries the same legal authority as a handwritten signature.
